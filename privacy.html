Next.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Next (also called Next Dawn) helps you move toward a goal one small step at a time. It is made by an independent developer based in Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps us run it, how long we keep it, and how you can see or delete it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect, and why

Your account: your email address and a sign-in id from Google Firebase. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address and sign-in id that Google or Apple share with us (with Apple you can choose a private relay address). Firebase keeps any password; we never see it. You can also start as a guest, with no email, and add one later.

Your plans: the goals you write, their steps, when you complete them, any done-by date you set, your answers to weekly check-ins, and a short history of changes you can undo (for example a step you removed). This is what the app is for.

Email check: if you sign up with an email and password we email you a 6-digit code. We keep a scrambled (hashed) copy of the code until it is used, and a record that your email was confirmed.

Premium: if you subscribe, we keep whether Premium is active and until when, and whether you have used the free week. We never receive your card or bank details: payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Feedback you choose to send us, linked to your account so it can be deleted with it.

Daily email, only if you turn it on: your email address, the time of day you picked and your time zone, so we can send one email a day with your next step. If you take a break in the app, we also keep the last day of that break so no email is sent until it is over. The email contains your goal's title and that step. You can stop it in Settings or with the link in every email, and it stops by itself after 14 days without a completed step.

Anonymous usage: which screens and buttons get used (for example "a step was completed"), with times and counts. These events never contain what you write, and they are stored under a scrambled id rather than your account id. You can turn this off in Settings > Share anonymous usage.

Website visits: on the web version, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and visits. It uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors.

Limits and reliability: a daily count of your AI requests (kept for 3 days, to stop overuse) and a log of whether AI requests worked and how long they took (no text and no user id, kept for 90 days).

What stays on your phone

Your answers to the setup questions (goal, time per day, what is getting in the way, preferred time of day, and, if you choose to give them, your first name, your reason for starting and what you wrote about yourself), your reminder settings, when you like to do your step (for example "after lunch"), the dates of any breaks you take, a copy of your current plan so the app works offline, and small settings. Your first name and your reason for starting are never sent to our server. Signing out or deleting your account clears this data from the phone.

Phone reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are off unless you turn them on.

What you write about yourself or about what is getting in the way is sent to the AI with your goal so the steps fit you. It is not saved on our servers.

Who helps us run Next

We share information only with the service providers below, and only what each needs to do its job:

Google Firebase Authentication: sign-in. Google and Apple: if you choose to sign in with them.

Google Gemini (AI): to suggest and adjust steps, we send Gemini your goal, the step being changed, steps you have already finished, your setup answers, anything you write about what is getting in the way, and what you wrote about yourself, if anything. We do not send your name, email or account id. While Next uses Google's free Gemini tier, Google's terms allow it to use that content to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. Please don't put sensitive details (health, money, other people's names) in your goals.

MongoDB Atlas: stores your plans. Render: runs our server.

RevenueCat: manages Premium subscriptions. It receives your account id and your purchase records from Apple or Google, but no card details.

An email delivery service (currently Brevo or Gmail): sends your verification code and, if you turn it on, the daily email.

Sentry (crash reporting, if turned on): crash reports are stripped of anything you typed, your account details and request contents before they are sent.

These providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We choose providers that protect data with recognised security standards.

What we don't do

No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell your data or share it for marketing.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with providers that encrypt it at rest. Access to our database is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

How long we keep it

Your account and plans are kept until you delete them. Verification codes are deleted within about an hour of expiring. AI counts are deleted after 3 days and the AI reliability log after 90 days. Deleting your account deletes everything linked to it on our servers straight away; copies in our providers' backups expire on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

Settings > Erase my progress deletes all your goals, steps and history but keeps your account.

Settings > Delete my account permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it on our servers: goals, steps, history, feedback, usage events, Premium and free-week records, your daily email setting and verification records. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings first.

Settings > Share anonymous usage turns usage events off.

You can ask to see, correct or get a copy of the information we hold about you by emailing next.dawn151@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, for example under the GDPR in the EU or UK; email us and we will help.

If you are not happy with how we handled a privacy concern, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or your local data protection authority.

Age

Next is for people aged 18 and over. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a person under 18 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: next.dawn151@gmail.com.

If anything here is unclear, or you think we have got something wrong, email next.dawn151@gmail.com and we will reply.
