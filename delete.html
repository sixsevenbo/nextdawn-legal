Delete your account

In the app: open Next, go to Settings > Delete my account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it on our servers: goals, steps, history, feedback, usage events and Premium records.

Without the app: email next.dawn151@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings first.
